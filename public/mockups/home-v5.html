What changed, and why

The page had no anchor. Cream on cream, every element the same weight, so the eye had nowhere to land. The forest band gives the top of the page somewhere to start, and the cards now overlap up into it — that overlap is what stops it reading as a stack of flat boxes.

Round beats rectangular for a percentage. A ring is a share of a whole, which is exactly what 75% of a goal is, and the notch on the ring is where you should be today. One shape now carries the number, the progress and the pace, replacing a bar, a tick, a floating caption and two chips.

Grey means disabled everywhere else in software. "Close Seed Round" is on pace — the good outcome — and today it renders in the grey we use for deactivated things. Colour encodes health, not degree: green for ahead and on pace, amber for behind, red for at risk.

Width is used, not stretched. Goals sit in an auto grid that fits two or three per row instead of one 1150px card with a void in the middle, and This Week and P&D drop to a full-width pair below rather than a tall thin column next to a short one.

Every card has a door. The note is an inset block you can click, and a goal with no update shows "Add the first update" instead of dead italic text. Toggle History → None yet to confirm nothing fabricates a trend line when there is no history.
