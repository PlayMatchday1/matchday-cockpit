Mockup data note. The Aug 3–9 grid is transcribed from the live Master Schedule screenshot: Atlanta 5 slots, Austin 62 slots of which 49 are on MatchDay and 13 are in Clubhouse only (62 − 13 = 49, the count the live header prints). Every session is its own cell here — the live page collapses two sessions at one slot into a single ×2 row, which hides the fact that the two can have different sources. Times are re-sorted chronologically; the live page puts 9 AM after 8 PM on Saturday.
Not from the screenshot: booking counts for future slots (the live build appends booked/capacity once a MatchDay match exists), and the MatchDay only state, which has no example in current data because the page has no such state yet — it renders as a 2px green #12704a border, a MatchDay only chip, and an Add to Clubhouse button. That is the only action anywhere on this page, because MatchDay cannot be written to from Clubhouse; Clubhouse-only slots are flagged and nothing more. Completed days render exactly as in the Slate Review mockup, so they are not repeated here. The city filter lists the two cities legible in the screenshot; the live page has seven.
