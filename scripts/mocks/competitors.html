Format
MD Standard is bookable spots divided by 18, one MatchDay match being 18 spots. It is the only figure comparable across formats: a 5v5 slot and an 11v11 slot are not the same match. Matches per week was captured but does not reconcile with the formats at several facilities, so it is not shown and is not the number to compare on.
